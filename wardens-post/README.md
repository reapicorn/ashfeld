# Warden's Post

The Warden's Post was not designed as an institution. It was a guardhouse — a single post at the edge of Ashfeld where the warden on duty reviewed the papers of anyone arriving from outside. No formal protocol. No central system. The warden decided.

The volume grew. People arrived with credentials issued by cities Ashfeld had never signed anything with. They arrived with tokens nobody in the city knew how to read. The warden kept deciding, but started writing the rules down so the next shift could do the same.

Those rules accumulated. They became policy. The post became an institution.

When the guilds collapsed, the Warden's Post kept operating. It had never depended on the guilds — it had never depended on anyone in particular. It kept receiving whoever arrived from outside, kept evaluating their credentials against the rules written on the wall, kept deciding under what conditions they could enter and what parts of the city they could reach.

Nobody reconstituted it. Nobody closed it. The warden on duty is still there.
